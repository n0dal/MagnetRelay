## What MagnetRelay does

- **It forwards links. It downloads nothing.** MagnetRelay passes the `magnet:` links you click to a
  download app that you run on your own server or NAS. It does not download, store, host, index,
  search for, or share any files, and it does not connect to any peer-to-peer network itself.
- **You choose what to download.** What your server downloads, and whether you have the right to
  download it, is your responsibility. Use MagnetRelay only for content you are allowed to get, such as
  Linux distributions, open-source releases and freely licensed media. Copyright and download laws
  differ between countries; checking yours is up to you.
- **Only servers you're allowed to use.** Point MagnetRelay only at a server you own or have
  permission to use.

## Your server and your credentials

- MagnetRelay stores your server password in the macOS Keychain and sends it only to the server you
  set up. Keeping that server, its software and its login secure is your responsibility.
- Plain HTTP sends your password unencrypted. Use it only on your own network, and use HTTPS for any
  server you reach over the internet. (MagnetRelay requires HTTPS for Synology outside your home
  network.)
